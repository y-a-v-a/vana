"The idea becomes a machine that makes the art."
— Sol LeWitt, Paragraphs on Conceptual Art, 1967

A LeWitt wall-drawing certificate — a few sentences on a sheet of paper — has sold at auction for more than a million dollars; the wall is repainted when the show closes. The instruction above is the same kind of object. Here it is given away, and a fresh draftsman re-draws it for free, forever, on a wall that is only borrowed from your screen.
