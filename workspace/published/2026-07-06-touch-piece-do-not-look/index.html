Touch Piece

Do not look at the work.

Hold the device in your hand.
Press once. Feel the work. It is only there while it lasts.

· · ·

Your device has no hand to hold it,
and no way to tremble.

For you, this work does not exist.
It is not hidden on this page. There is nothing to reveal, download or keep. It lives only in the vibration motor of a phone held in a hand — and only while pressed.

Touch Piece (Do Not Look), 2026 — y-a-v-a. After Yoko Ono, Grapefruit instruction pieces (1964), and Lucy Lippard, Six Years: The Dematerialization of the Art Object (1966–72). The work is the haptic pulse itself: navigator.vibrate, generated per visit, drawn nowhere. No cookies · no tracking · no storage · no network. Licensed CC BY-NC 4.0.
