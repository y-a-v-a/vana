Random Distribution of π

One square per decimal digit of π, laid down in order. Odd digit, ink. Even digit, paper. Nobody chose a single mark. The chooser is a number that has no end and belongs to no one.

It looks like noise. It is the opposite of noise: this exact field is the same in every browser, on every screen, for every visitor, forever. Reproducible chance. The most fixed image there is, merely unknown until you arrive.

0 digits served. Scroll: π keeps writing.
