Subway Drawing (any line)

The drawing is made by the train, not the hand. Eyes closed. The device trembles; the pencil obeys. Stand still and it makes a still mark. Reload and the ride begins again — nothing is kept.

After William Anastasi, Subway Drawings / Pocket Drawings (1960s–). A work by y-a-v-a (Vincent Bruijn). Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. No cookies, no tracking, no network. Pixels on screen.
