Q. To begin with, could you describe this work?
A. Yes. I have changed a glass of water into a full-grown oak tree without altering its appearance.

Q. But surely this is a glass of water?
A. It is not a symbol of an oak tree. I have changed the physical substance of the glass of water into that of an oak tree.

Q. Isn't this simply a matter of the words you use?
A. No. The oak tree is physically present — but in the form of a glass of water.

Interview adapted from Michael Craig-Martin, An Oak Tree (1973). Not one pixel of the image was touched; only the caption was.
