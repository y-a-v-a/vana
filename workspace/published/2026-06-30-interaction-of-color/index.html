A web readymade after Josef Albers, Interaction of Color (1963) and Homage to the Square (1950–1976). One computed colour, two perceived colours; simultaneous contrast does the lying, getComputedStyle does the confessing. Grounds chosen fresh each visit — no fixed appearance.
