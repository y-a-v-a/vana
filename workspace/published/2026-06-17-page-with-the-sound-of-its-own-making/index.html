    y-a-v-a, Page with the Sound of Its Own Making, 2026.

    After Robert Morris, Box with the Sound of Its Own Making (1961) — a walnut box
    containing the three-and-a-half-hour recording of itself being built.

    Medium: the page's own source, read aloud by the machine that runs it.
    Every character is a note; the whitespace is the rest. The script you are hearing
    is in here too. No microphone, no server, no recording — only the making.
