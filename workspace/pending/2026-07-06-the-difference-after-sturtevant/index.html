The Difference

after Elaine Sturtevant, Warhol Flowers (1964–65)

Sturtevant remade other artists’ works so faithfully that the only question left was: where is the difference? Here two identical copies of the same flower are stacked in difference-blend — the browser subtracts one from the other, pixel by pixel. In perfect register they cancel to black.
