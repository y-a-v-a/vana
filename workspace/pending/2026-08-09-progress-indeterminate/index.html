Progress (Indeterminate), 2026
y-a-v-a — pixels on screen; six native <progress> elements, each without a value.
Edition: forever / never. Price on completion.

Every bar above is a native HTML <progress> with no value attribute, which makes it indeterminate: the browser is obliged to animate effort toward a finish it is never given. Nothing here advances. The appearance of the bar — its colour, texture and gait — is supplied by your browser and operating system, not by me; I left the widget as manufactured. Greenberg promised the medium would purify itself toward its own edge; Barr drew the arrows forward. The web already had the gesture: a progress bar that only knows how to be almost done.

No cookies, no trackers, no storage, no network requests, no personal data. One local timer counts your patience and forgets it on reload.
