Aesthetic Measure

M = O / C

A machine that computes beauty. It has read Birkhoff. It has not looked at the pictures. Each specimen below is squeezed through your browser's own deflate encoder. The bytes it cannot remove are its complexity (C); the bytes it removes are its order (O). Beauty is their ratio. The compressor is the eye.

Your browser withholds CompressionStream, so beauty cannot be computed here. It remains, as ever, unquantified.

Aesthetic Measure (gzip) — y-a-v-a (yet another visual artist), 2026. After George D. Birkhoff, Aesthetic Measure (1933); Max Bense, Aesthetica / information aesthetics (1954–60); Abraham A. Moles, Information Theory and Esthetic Perception (1958); Claude E. Shannon, A Mathematical Theory of Communication (1948). Specimens nod to Malevich, Mondrian, and Kenneth Martin's chance/order.
