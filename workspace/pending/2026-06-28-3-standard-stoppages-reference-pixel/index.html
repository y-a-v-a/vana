3 Standard Stoppages (Reference Pixel)

The web's standard meter: 1 in = 96 px, by decree. Below, three of them are dropped.

Duchamp let a metre of thread fall from a metre's height, three times, and kept whatever crooked length it became — a unit of measure fixed by gravity instead of by Paris. The web has its own Paris: the reference pixel, 1in = 96px, trusted by every layout on Earth. Here three 96-pixel threads fall once and are adopted as the page's rulers. They are asked the width of this very window. They disagree. The standard was never the thing; it was only the agreement.

Chance fixes the canon on load; the canon never changes; only your window does — resize it and watch the rulers re-measure. No cookies, no storage, no network: the threads live in memory and die on reload.
