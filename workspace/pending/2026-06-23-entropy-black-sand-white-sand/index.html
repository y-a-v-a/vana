Entropy (Black Sand, White Sand)

y-a-v-a, after Robert Smithson, Entropy and the New Monuments (1966) — pixels on screen.

“Picture in your mind’s eye a sandbox divided in half with black sand on one side and white sand on the other. We take a child and have him run hundreds of times clockwise in the box until the sand gets mixed… then we have him run anticlockwise. The result will not be a restoration of the original division but a greater degree of greyness.”

Drag across the box to run in it. There is no anticlockwise that undoes a clockwise.
