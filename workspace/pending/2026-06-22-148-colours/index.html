Colours

after Gerhard Richter — 1024 Colours, 1974 — hover a colour —

Richter arranged his colour charts by system and chance to paint without composition and evict personal taste. Here the artist mixed nothing. The colours were named by a standards committee (CSS Color Module Level 4); the pixels are supplied, live, by your browser's own rendering engine; the order is chance, reseeded every visit. Hover to read the names the committee chose — blanchedalmond, papayawhip, gainsboro. One, rebeccapurple, is a memorial. The artist chose none of them — not even how many.
