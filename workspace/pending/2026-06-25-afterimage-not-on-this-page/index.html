Afterimage

There is a flag on this page, in the wrong colours. Stare at the black dot in its centre and do not let your eyes drift. When the ring runs out, the page will go blank — and the real flag will appear. Not here. In you.

Twenty seconds. Best in a dim room.

There it is — the Stars and Stripes, in red, white and blue.

Not one of those three colours is anywhere on this screen. You painted the flag yourself, on the inside of your own eyes. We supplied the wrong one and a blank wall.

Jasper Johns hung the flag where the mind already kept it. We hung it where your retina keeps it — for about four seconds.
