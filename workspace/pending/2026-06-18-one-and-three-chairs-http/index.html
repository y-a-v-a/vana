One and Three Chairs (HTTP)

after Joseph Kosuth, One and Three Chairs, 1965 — the web edition, in which the chair never arrives.

Kosuth could wheel the chair into the gallery. We can’t. So here is its picture, its definition, and an empty element where the object would be — which, on the web, is the whole truth of it.
