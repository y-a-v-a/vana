Theaters (The Whole Film at Once), 2026. Pixels on screen, single continuous exposure. Running time: as long as you watch. Edition: one per visit, never the same twice.

A film is projected onto the screen — every frame a different scene — and the screen keeps all of it, holding the running average of every frame it has ever shown. Sugimoto left the shutter open for an entire feature and the movie erased itself into white. Here the shutter never closes: the more you are shown, the less there is to see. Sit through to the end and the end is a blank.
