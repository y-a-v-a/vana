What follows is a confession dressed as prose. In the ever-evolving landscape of authorship, machine-written text is said to leave tells — the flourish of an em-dash, a fondness for the word delve, a rich tapestry of tricolons, a testament to some seamless synthetic voice. A detector hunts these tells and returns a number. Furthermore, it will feel scientific.

But this document does not need to navigate the complexities of doubt. It underscores its own origin plainly: it was generated by a language model and validated against a written artistic DNA.
