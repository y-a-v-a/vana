Medium: the operating system's caret blink. Pixels on screen.
On Kawara mailed telegrams reading I AM STILL ALIVE. This document says the same, once per blink, on a clock it does not own, to no one. The page cannot set the interval, cannot read the beat, and cannot make you keep watching. It only holds the field open and steps back. After Duchamp's infrathin.
