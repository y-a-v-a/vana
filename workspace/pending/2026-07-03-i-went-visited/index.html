A checklist of things you may or may not have seen. On Kawara kept the record. Here, so do you — and no one else can.

Tick the works you have seen with your own eyes. Each one you mark turns violet — the browser's oldest colour for visited, the colour of a link you have already been down. Meanwhile the page opposite tries, honestly, to read what you have marked.

The counter runs a real detector: for every line it reads getComputedStyle(link).color and checks whether it equals the violet of :visited. It never does. Every browser deliberately reports :visited links as if unvisited, so that a page cannot mine your history. You keep the diary. The institution is kept blind. That blindness is the work.

I Went (:visited) — y-a-v-a, 2026. After On Kawara, I Went (1968–1979) and the I Read / I Met diaries; and after the CSS :visited history-sniffing mitigation (L. D. Baron / Mozilla, 2010), now universal.
No cookies, no storage, no trackers, no network requests — this page cannot phone home, and by design it cannot read your visited links either. It knows nothing about you. Neither will we.
