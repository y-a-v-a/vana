GanzfeldJames Turrell, restaged as a browser security feature

A single, edgeless field of light. Step in and the browser takes your cursor, reports only how far you move — never where you are — and refuses to draw an edge. You can travel forever and arrive nowhere.

Any drift or shimmer you notice happens in your visual cortex, not in the pixels — it is deprived of edges and invents them. The exit is not yours to choose: the browser keeps Esc for itself.
