Verb List — Actions to Relate to a Page

After Richard Serra, who wrote a column of transitive verbs and called it sculpture. Here the verbs are not described. They are performed — by the only thing that can actually to twist, to smear, to scatter a real material: the rendering engine. The page is the material. The score runs on its own body.
