Round as Giotto’s O

Vasari tells that the Pope’s messenger asked the painters of Italy for a drawing, to judge who was worthiest. Giotto, resting his elbow against his side to make a compass of his arm, turned his hand once and left a circle so perfectly round it became a proverb. Draw yours in a single stroke — no lifting.
