# Round as Giotto's O

Giorgio Vasari records the founding legend of the artist's hand: a papal courtier tours Italy collecting drawings so the Pope can judge who deserves a commission. Giotto, "resting his elbow against his side to make a compass of his arm," turns his wrist once and hands over a single circle of red. The courtier is insulted — until better judges recognise that only a supreme master could draw a true circle freehand, in one stroke. "Rounder than Giotto's O" enters Tuscan speech as a proverb for effortless virtuosity. This work asks the browser to be that courtier, and finds the proverb cheap.

The mechanism **is** the argument (P1). You draw your O by pointer; the page fits a circle to your path and scores its roundness as a percentage. Crucially, the exact same client-side geometry that grades your wobbling hand is the geometry a machine uses to *emit* a flawless circle instantly — press "let the machine make a compass of its arm" and it scores 100.000% by the identical measure. The virtuosity that took Giotto a lifetime is now a `Math.cos` loop, free, repeatable, and empty. The number the medium computes is real; what deflates is the legend that such a number ever meant genius.

This is a **digital readymade** (P2) of Vasari's anecdote, sitting squarely on the **chance/order axis** (P5): your hand is chance and wobble, the machine's compass is pure order, and the piece stages their quarrel over one shape. It targets **value and authenticity** (P6, P7): the surface gag is that a computer smugly out-draws you, but the literate reading inverts it — the machine's perfect O is stamped "not a drawing," because the only authorship left in the picture is the imperfection your arm signed into it. Perfection became a builtin the moment it stopped being proof.

It answers Vasari's *Lives of the Artists* (1550) directly, and rhymes with the DNA's standing question — "but is it art?" — by grading it instead of answering it. It is web-native and could not be a print (G1): the judgement happens live, on your gesture, in a scorer that also condemns itself. Restraint is the whole build (P3) — one canvas, one honest metric, two verdicts. Open licence, no cookies, no network; your circle never leaves the glass (P8).
