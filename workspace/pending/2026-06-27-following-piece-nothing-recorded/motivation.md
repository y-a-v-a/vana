# Following Piece (Nothing Recorded)

In 1969 Vito Acconci spent a month picking a random stranger on the street and following them until they stepped into a private space he could not enter — a car, a doorway — at which point the piece, for that subject, was over. The duration and the route belonged entirely to the followed person; the artist surrendered authorship to the act of pursuit. This page re-stages that work with the one tool the web already built for following people: the pointer event stream.

The mechanism *is* the argument (P1). `pointermove` is the exact primitive on which the entire mouse-tracking, heatmap, session-replay, surveillance-capitalism economy is built — every analytics vendor watches your cursor with this event. Here that same event drives a follower that eases toward you at a discreet lag (Acconci's "at a distance"), stands with you when you stop, and ends the follow the moment your cursor leaves the frame — you have "entered private space." Crucially, the page writes nothing: no cookie, no `localStorage`, no `fetch`, no log. The follow that powers all web tracking is performed here stripped of its memory. The on-screen report reads **recorded: nothing · 0 bytes** as a standing fact, and the "subjects followed" tally resets to zero on reload because it, too, is held only in a closure that dies with the tab.

This sits where the catalogue lives: a clean surface gag (a thing chases my cursor) over a precise argument (P7) — Acconci's transfer of control to the subject, and the inversion of analytics into an amnesiac, GDPR-perfect stalker. It answers a named conceptual work (Acconci, *Following Piece*, 1969) and could not be a print: the piece has no fixed image, only a behaviour produced live by the viewer's own movement (G1, P4). It runs on the order/chance axis (P5) — a deterministic easing pursuit driven by the pure chance of where a human hand wanders.

The voice is the cheerful cynic (P9, G5): the wall label calls it "the only ethical stalker," which is both the joke and the thesis — the technology of following is not the violation; the *keeping* is. Open licence, no attribution to harvest, nothing leaves the page (P8). Restraint over spectacle: one easing follower, one sightline, one honest ledger that always reads zero (P3).
