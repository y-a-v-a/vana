House

This is a room. It is built from the space between these words — the margins, the line gaps, the emptiness you were trained to read straight past.

Press the button. It fills that emptiness with concrete, then pulls the room away. What is left is the cast: the air made solid, the text made hole. The measurements are taken from wherever your browser happens to lay this out, so no two visitors are demolished the same way.

Rachel Whiteread cast the inside of a Victorian terrace in 1993; then the terrace was demolished and only the cast stood in the street. This one demolishes on click and heals on reload. Nothing is kept.
