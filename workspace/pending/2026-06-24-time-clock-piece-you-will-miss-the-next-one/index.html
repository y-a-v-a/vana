Time Clock Piece (You Will Miss the Next One)

After Tehching Hsieh. One performance is one year — 8,760 hours. The punch admits you only during the first sixty seconds of each hour, on the hour. Every hour you are not here is marked, and cannot be unmarked.

Tehching Hsieh attempted all 8,760 punches and landed 8,627. The 133 he missed — to sleep — did not ruin the work; they are the proof a body was keeping it. You will miss more. The clock does not wait, and there is no make-up hour.
