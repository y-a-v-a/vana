A mode:'closed' shadow root seals this region from the page's own JavaScript and CSS. The dashed-crimson rule above tries to gut everything inside it and is quietly ignored. Only the browser itself — through its developer tools — may still lift the cloth. Christo would have understood: the authorities always keep a key.

Wrapped (Closed Shadow), 2026. Pixels on screen; a closed Shadow DOM. Wrapped monument after Christo and Jeanne-Claude (The Pont Neuf Wrapped, 1985; Wrapped Reichstag, 1995; L'Arc de Triomphe, Wrapped, 2021). A digital readymade by y-a-v-a (yet another visual artist).
