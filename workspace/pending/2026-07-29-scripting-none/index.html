The gallery is closed to running browsers.

This work is on view only while scripting is switched off. Yours is on.

Note what did not happen: no script ran to tell you this. There is no JavaScript served on this page at all. Your stylesheet asked your browser a single question — @media (scripting) — and your browser admitted that, if asked, it would run whatever it was handed.

That admission was enough. You are turned away not for anything you did, but for what you were prepared to execute.

To enter: disable JavaScript for this page and reload. Disarm the machine, and the picture appears.
