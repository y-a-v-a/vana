La Disparition (A Void). In 1969 Georges Perec wrote a 300-page novel without once using the letter E — the most common letter in the French language, and in this one. The missing letter was not a game alone: E stands in for eux, "them", the family the war took from him. Here the constraint is not asked of you; it is enforced. The browser intercepts every keystroke before it lands, and the fifth letter never arrives. You feel the void by reaching for it.
