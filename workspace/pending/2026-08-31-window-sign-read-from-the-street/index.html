Window Sign (Read from the Street) — y-a-v-a, 2026. After Bruce Nauman, neon window/spiral signs (e.g. The True Artist Helps the World by Revealing Mystic Truths, 1967), read forward from inside and backward from the street. Mechanism: the marquee is stored in the DOM reversed; CSS unicode-bidi: bidi-override; direction: rtl turns it to face your eye, while the clipboard, Find-in-page, crawlers and screen readers get the raw source — the reversed back of the glass. See also the Unicode Bidirectional Algorithm and the Trojan Source reversal attack (Boucher & Anderson, 2021); and, inverted, y-a-v-a Scrittura Speculare (2026), where the eye sees the mirror and the machine reads it straight.
