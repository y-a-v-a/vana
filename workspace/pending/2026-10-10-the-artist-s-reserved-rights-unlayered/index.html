The Artist's Reserved Rights (Unlayered)

Who has the last word over how the work looks — the artist, or the order of the cascade?

The artist named the work as precisely as the language allows: figure.frame .work#piece — specificity (1,1,1). The market named it .work — specificity (0,1,0).

By every measure of specificity the artist wins. The square is grey anyway, because the market's rule sits in a layer declared after the artist's, and a later layer beats a more specific rule in an earlier one. Ownership is not specificity. It is order of declaration.

Only a rule written outside every layer can overrule the market — an instrument from beyond the ordinary order of sale. In 1971 Seth Siegelaub and Robert Projansky drafted exactly such an instrument. Tick the box to file it. Almost no one ever did.
