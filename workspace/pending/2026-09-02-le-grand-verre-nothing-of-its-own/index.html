Le Grand Verre — Nothing of Its Own

Everything in the glass is the room, delayed.
After Marcel Duchamp, The Bride Stripped Bare by Her Bachelors, Even (The Large Glass), 1915–1923 — a delay in glass, shattered in transit in 1927 and, by chance, completed. Here the two panes carry no colour and no image: their only pixels are a backdrop-filter of whatever stands behind them. The cracks are re-thrown per visit by chance (crypto.getRandomValues).
