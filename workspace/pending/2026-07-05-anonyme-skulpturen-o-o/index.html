Anonyme Skulpturen / Anonymous Sculptures

Wassertürme, o. O., o. J. — water towers, no location, no date. Photographed frontally, at a fixed distance, and gridded so the eye compares.

Bernd & Hilla Becher travelled Europe for fifty years photographing water towers, gas holders and winding towers before they were demolished, presenting them in deadpan grids: an archive against forgetting.
