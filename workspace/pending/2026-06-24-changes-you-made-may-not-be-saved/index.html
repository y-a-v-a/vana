Changes you made
may not be saved.

When you try to leave this page, I will try to tell you:

 

The browser will not allow it. In my place, it will say only:

Leave site? Changes you made may not be saved.

Your browser may phrase it differently — but never in my words. Around 2016 every major browser quietly removed the artist's ability to write a farewell into this moment, to stop the message from being abused. The gesture is still permitted. The words are not. So I hand mine over each time you go, and each time they are thrown away and replaced with a sentence about saving.

y-a-v-a (Vincent Bruijn) — Changes You Made May Not Be Saved, 2026
Medium: the beforeunload event; pixels on screen
After Bas Jan Ader, I'm too sad to tell you (1971), and after y-a-v-a, i-m-too-sad-to-tell-you (2012) — the artist who could not speak, now gagged.
A new farewell is chosen at random on every visit. Nothing is stored, sent, or remembered.
