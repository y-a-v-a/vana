A found sentence, framed. The quotation marks — the only thing that turns a line into a quotation, i.e. someone else's — are not written in the page. Your browser prints them, in your nation's punctuation, from the CSS quotes property. Select the sentence and copy it: the marks fall off, and you carry away plain, unauthored text. The frame was never yours to keep.

After Sherrie Levine, After Walker Evans (1981); Marcel Duchamp / the readymade; Roland Barthes, The Death of the Author (1967) — “a tissue of quotations”.
