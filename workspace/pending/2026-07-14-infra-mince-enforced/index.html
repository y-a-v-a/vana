Infra-mince (Enforced) — y-a-v-a, 2026. After Marcel Duchamp, inframince (notes, c. 1935).

Medium: elapsed time, quantized by your browser for your protection. It rounds performance.now() so this page cannot use the clock to fingerprint you or read your neighbours' secrets — the Spectre / Meltdown mitigation of 2018, still standing. The number above is the price your vendor set on the smallest difference. Below its teeth, two distinct instants are declared the same instant.

Duchamp called the smallest conceivable difference the infra-mince — the warmth left in a chair, the two faces of a sheet of paper. Here it is a hard floor, measured live on your machine, different on the next. Nothing is stored, nothing is sent: measuring you precisely is exactly what has been forbidden.
