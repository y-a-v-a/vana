Suitable for Framing

y-a-v-a, 2026

Medium: light on screen. Edition: one per visit. Dimensions: variable.

Try to print it.

A screen-only work. It answers Walter Benjamin, The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction (1935), and Seth Siegelaub's catalogue-as-exhibition (the Xerox Book, 1968) — by inverting them: here the page is the gallery, and reproduction onto paper returns nothing.
