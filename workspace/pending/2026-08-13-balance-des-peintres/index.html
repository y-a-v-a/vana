Balance des Peintres

Roger de Piles weighed the great painters on four qualities, twenty points each, in 1708. Three centuries later the browser reads his numbers and paints, of its own accord, which masters are green and which are red.

Scores from Roger de Piles, Cours de peinture par principes, avec un balance des peintres (Paris, 1708). De Piles set 20 at perfection, then declared it unreachable — so the gauges can never fill, and no painter is ever entirely green. Caravaggio's Expression stands at 0.

No script runs on this page. The bars, their fill, and the colour of the verdict — green where a number clears the vendor's threshold, red where it falls short — are drawn entirely by your browser's native <meter> element. The taste is nobody's.
