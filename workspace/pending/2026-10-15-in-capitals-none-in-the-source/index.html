In Capitals (None in the Source)

y-a-v-a · 2026 · pixels on screen · CSS text-transform + the clipboard

Capital letters are the typography of permanence: the Roman inscription, the lapidary frieze, the donor's name endowed in perpetuity, the all-caps authority of the headline. Here that authority is a render pass. The browser lifts lowercase source into monumental capitals; the clipboard and the DOM keep only the whisper. Horace promised a monument more lasting than bronze. This one is less lasting than Ctrl-C.

After: Horace, Odes III.30 (aere perennius, 23 BC); Trajan's Column / Roman monumental capitals; Walter Benjamin, The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction (1935); net.art source-vs-render tradition (y-a-v-a: Silencio, La Disparition).
