MAGNET TV

put your finger on the tube — the field is yours to hold
Magnet TV (Bring Your Own Field) — pixels on screen, live, no signal recorded.
The set broadcasts nothing but its own test pattern — perfect order, the picture shown when there is nothing to show. Press and drag: your pointer is the magnet. The raster bends around the field you hold; lift your hand and broadcast order returns, unbruised. Nothing here is stored, so no distortion you make will ever exist again.
