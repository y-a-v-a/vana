Artist’s Statement

You do not write it. You choose it.

 

Every word the art world permits is already in the list below the cursor — the browser’s own dropdown, chrome you cannot restyle. Authorship here is not invention but selection from a finite, sanctioned lexicon. Nothing you type is saved, and nothing is sent; the field keeps no record and reaches no server.

After y-a-v-a, Modernipsum (2012), which generated the discourse; this one merely offers it back for approval. References: Alix Rule & David Levine, “International Art English” (Triple Canopy, 2012); Marcel Duchamp, the readymade.
