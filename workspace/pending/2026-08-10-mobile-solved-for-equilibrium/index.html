Mobile (Solved for Equilibrium)

No two moments alike. Nothing here is arranged — the pivots are forced by the law of the lever, the parts are thrown afresh on every visit, and it will not hold still. A screenshot would freeze the one thing a mobile is defined by refusing to be.
You have asked for stillness. Here is the corpse of the mobile: the print it was never meant to become.
