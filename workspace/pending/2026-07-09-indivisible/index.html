Indivisible

Three words that mean not to be broken. Each sits in a column too narrow to hold it, so the browser breaks it — at a point it alone chooses, with a hyphen that is not in the text.

indivisible
unbreakable
continuous

Indivisible — pixels on screen; the layout engine's own hyphenation dictionary. Per visit, per width, per declared language.
After Augusto de Campos & the Noigandres group, concrete poetry (the word as an object controlled in space, 1950s); Marcel Duchamp, infrathin (the whole word and the broken word).
