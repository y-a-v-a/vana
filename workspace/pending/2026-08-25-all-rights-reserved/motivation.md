# All Rights Reserved

A page titled *All Rights Reserved* guards its "masterpiece" with the one device the amateur web has always reached for: it disables right-click. Right-click anywhere and the browser's context menu never opens; instead a beveled Web-1.0 notice slides up — *"⚠ Protected — right-click has been disabled to protect the artwork"* — and a security ledger increments *Right-clicks intercepted*. Directly beneath it, a second ledger lists every route by which you may still take the work in full — View Source, screenshot, Ctrl/Cmd+S, select-and-copy, re-type by hand — each marked *∞, permitted*. The footer states the licence: Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0. The title forbids what the licence begs for.

The mechanism **is** the argument (P1). `contextmenu` + `preventDefault()` is a genuine, functioning lock — and it locks a menu, not a picture. It is the exact, literal enactment of Walter Benjamin's point in *The Work of Art in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction* (1935): the aura cannot be technically re-attached to an object that reproduces at zero cost. The counter that only ever counts intercepted *menus* — while *Reproductions prevented* sits frozen at 0 — is not a metaphor for futile protection; it is the futile protection, running live in your browser (P4). Nothing here would survive as a print: a print has no menu to disable, no source to view, no per-visit colour field.

The found object is the ritual itself (P2, P9): the "no right-click" script is a real relic of net.art-era folk paranoia (Lialina & Espenschied's *One Terabyte of Kilobyte Age* preserves thousands of such gestures), re-staged as a Duchampian readymade. It reads at two depths (P7): the casual visitor gets the deadpan gag of a lock guarding a coloured rectangle; the literate visitor gets a precise argument about aura, ownership, and DRM theatre. It targets value and authenticity head-on (P6) — "All Rights Reserved" is the loudest claim of ownership in the language, uttered over a work that has already given itself away.

The ethics are the punchline (P8): the open licence is not a compliance footnote but the co-star, printed on the same page whose title denies it. Kept to the smallest build that completes the thought (P3) — one event listener, no storage, no network — the work answers the reproduction thread in the y-a-v-a catalogue (*The Original*, *Untitled (Copy)*) from a new angle: not the copy that is free, but the lock that forbids nothing, still dutifully counting.
