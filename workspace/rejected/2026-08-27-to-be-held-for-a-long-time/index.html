A just perfect fifth — the fifth tuned to the exact ratio 3:2, so the two sine tones beat against nothing. It does not fade and there is no stop: the score gives none. Silence it by leaving the room — close or mute the tab. Nothing is stored; the next performance begins again at zero.

Sound. Begins only when you ask it to.

y-a-v-a — yet another visual artist. Realised after La Monte Young, Composition 1960 #7 (1960), and the eternal-drone / light environment of La Monte Young & Marian Zazeela, Dream House.
Two oscillators, one gain, no server, no cookies, no storage, no network. The tone lives only in the audio render loop of your browser.
