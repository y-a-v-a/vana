Everyone Is an Artist.

Jeder Mensch ist ein Künstler.

Joseph Beuys said it in the 1970s. The browser had already agreed and shipped it as document.designMode. Here it is, switched on for the whole document.

Every word on this page is now yours to change. Rewrite this sentence. Correct my grammar. Cross out the parts you dislike. Sign your own name over mine below. Edit the licence. Delete the entire work if that is your gesture — deletion is permitted, and it is still art if you say so.

There are no scripts listening. Nothing here reacts to you, judges you, or records you. The social sculpture is genuinely collective and completely amnesiac: the moment you reload, every hand's work — yours and mine — is forgotten, equally. An eternal monument with the memory of a goldfish.

y-a-v-a after Joseph Beuys, Soziale Plastik (Social Sculpture). Sign over me.
