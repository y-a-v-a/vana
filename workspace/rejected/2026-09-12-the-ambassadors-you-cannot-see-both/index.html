The Ambassadors — you cannot see both

angle 0°

Two ambassadors, their globe and their instruments, painted to be read from straight ahead. The grey streak across the floor is nothing — until you tilt the panel to a grazing angle, where it foreshortens into a skull and the men dissolve. The browser's own 3-D projection cannot render both truths at once: to look at death you must look at everything else wrong. y-a-v-a (Vincent Bruijn), after Hans Holbein the Younger, The Ambassadors (1533), and the anamorphosis of Jean-François Nicéron.
